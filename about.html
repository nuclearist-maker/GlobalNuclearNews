About GlobalNuclearNews

Nuclear news, reactor fundamentals and reference material in one place.

Our purpose

GlobalNuclearNews brings together nuclear industry news, reactor theory, a concept dictionary, nuclear history and reactor data. It is intended for readers who want to understand both current developments and the technical ideas behind them.

What you can explore

  • Global Nuclear News: developments in projects, regulation and technology.
  • Nuclear Theory and the Concept Dictionary: explanations of reactor systems and physical principles.
  • Nuclear History: people, discoveries and milestones.
  • Reactor Data: operating reactors and construction plans, with coverage and methodology notes.

Responsibility and limits

This site is maintained through the nuclearist-maker GitHub account. It is an educational publication, not a regulator, plant operator or source of operating instructions. For engineering, licensing or safety decisions, consult the applicable primary documents and qualified professionals.
