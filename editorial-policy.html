Editorial Policy

Our approach to sources, AI assistance and corrections.

Sources and interpretation

News and reference articles draw on published sources such as regulators, international organizations, project developers, technical literature and historical records. References may appear as links or bibliographic text. Readers should consult the source and its publication date when checking a claim.

Project announcements, applications, approvals, construction and commercial operation are different stages. Announced schedules and capacities are subject to change. Reactor data pages explain their coverage and limitations separately.

AI assistance

AI tools assist with research organization, drafting, translation, illustrations and website production. AI output can contain errors. Its use does not establish that a statement is correct or that every existing article has undergone independent human expert review. Editorial responsibility remains with the site operator.

Corrections

Readers can request a correction through the Contact page. Please identify the article, the disputed statement and supporting evidence. Corrections should distinguish an error in the original account from a later change in a project's status.

Use of this publication

The site provides educational explanations and commentary. It is not a substitute for current licensing documents, plant procedures, engineering calculations or professional advice. Illustrations simplify concepts and are not construction or operating drawings.

Advertising and attribution

Advertising is not currently enabled. Source citations identify where information came from; they do not imply endorsement by the cited organization. Any future paid placement should be clearly identified and kept distinguishable from editorial content.
